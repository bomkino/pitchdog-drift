# Creator journey

Drift should reward direction, not familiarity with renderer architecture.

## Slides → World → Direct → Master

1. **Slides** — import the deck, repair ordering, and read source-health warnings before styling it.
2. **World** — choose one authored film world. A world owns motion, atmosphere, surface and optics together.
3. **Direct** — choose a pressure level, generate non-compounding takes, then tune only decisions that matter.
4. **Master** — choose the publishing frame, inspect guides, set the poster moment, resolve readiness blockers, and export.

`⌘/Ctrl + K` or `?` opens Director Commands. It accepts creator language: “variation”, “before after”, “social safe”, “output”, “undo”.

## What Drift protects

- **Legibility:** readability pressure names aggressive velocity, smear, colour separation, defocus, bend and frame reduction without pretending to read the slide.
- **Source truth:** mixed ratios and insufficient source resolution remain visible. Drift suggests repairs; it does not silently rewrite the deck.
- **Reversibility:** undo/redo coalesces a gesture into one decision. New takes do not compound mutations. Clean comparison is temporary.
- **Export truth:** transparent H.264, missing slides, unavailable encoders and unverifiable 50/60 fps presenter audio fail before a picker opens.
- **Accessibility:** the command surface is searchable, keyboard navigable, focus contained, screen-reader announced and visibly discoverable.
- **Determinism:** poster moments, seamless motion, procedural worlds and optics use the same evaluated scene in preview and export.

## Deliberate boundaries

Drift does not auto-design a deck, infer story meaning from pixels, or pretend maximum cinematic pressure is tasteful. Moving-track media remains image-first. One pinned presenter video keeps decoding, export timing and failure states legible.
