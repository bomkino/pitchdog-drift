# Code of conduct

This project should be a place where people can do ambitious work without reproducing the bullying, exclusion, extraction, and status games common in creative and technical industries.

## Expected

- Critique the work concretely; do not diminish the person.
- Make room for people with less institutional power, confidence, English fluency, or free time.
- Credit labour and sources.
- Respect privacy, names, pronouns, boundaries, and consent.
- Accept correction without turning it into a trial of the person who raised harm.
- Prefer repair, clarity, and material safety over reputation management.

## Unacceptable

Harassment, stalking, threats, sexualised attention, discriminatory language, deliberate misgendering, doxxing, retaliation, plagiarism, exploitative requests for unpaid labour, and sustained bad-faith disruption are not welcome in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate these boundaries. Serious or repeated harm may result in a temporary or permanent ban. Enforcement decisions should protect affected people, avoid public spectacle, and document material conflicts of interest.

For a private report, use GitHub’s private vulnerability/reporting surfaces when applicable or contact `hello@pitch.dog`. Do not include confidential client media unless a maintainer explicitly provides a secure route.
